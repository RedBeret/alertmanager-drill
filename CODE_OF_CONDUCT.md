# Code of conduct

## Standard

Be civil and assume good faith. Critique the code, the design, or the evidence, not the
person who wrote it.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, and publishing someone's
private information without permission.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other space where someone is
representing this project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem through GitHub on this repository. Reports are handled privately.
Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that do not meet this standard, and
may block a contributor who continues after a warning.
